Welcome, dance families
Before you start
The Parent Portal is the studio's home for class schedules, announcements and dates that matter. It lives at didc.app and works on any phone — nothing to download from an app store.
// All you need
  • Your phone (or a tablet or computer — everything works there too).
  • The email address the studio has for you — the one on your Enrollio account.
You make an account once
The portal now has its own login, so what you see is your family — your dancers, your classes, your announcements. Making the account takes about three minutes and only happens once. It is separate from your Enrollio password, but it starts from the same email address. If the studio ever gave you a studio code, it is no longer used.
// The 60-second version
  1. 1Open didc.app and tap Dancer Portal.
  2. 2Tap Sign up the first time — after that, Log in.
  3. 3Your home screen opens with your dancers on it, and the studio's sections along the top.

The rest of this packet walks through each of those, screen by screen.

Two programs, one portal
All-Star Dancers is the competition team side; Academy / TNT Dancers is the class side. Both work exactly the same way — this guide uses All-Stars in the pictures, but every step applies to both.
Nothing to install. It's a website that behaves like an app — always the latest version, nothing to update.
Use it on any device. The same login works on your phone, a tablet and a computer — sign in on as many as you like.
Always current. Schedules and dates come straight from the studio — what you see is what the front desk sees.

First time only · about three minutes
Making your account
Everything starts at didc.app. You need the email address the studio already has for you — the one on your Enrollio account.
  1. 1Open your phone's browser and go to didc.app. Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android — either is fine.
  2. 2Tap the pink Dancer Portal tile. Staff Portal is for teachers and front-desk staff. If you end up there, go back and pick the other tile.
  3. 3Tap Sign up. Log in is the one you'll use every time after today — it needs an account to exist first.
  4. 4Type your first name, last name and your Enrollio email address, then tap Continue. Any other address is not recognised, and no code is sent.
  5. 5Choose a password and type it twice. Six characters or more — let your phone save it.
  6. 6Fetch the 6-digit code from your email, type it in, and you're in. After today it's just Log in.

One-time setup · optional but worth it
Put it on your phone
Add DIDC to your home screen and it opens like a regular app — no address to type, and it still works when the signal is poor.
On iPhone
Using Safari
  1. 1Open didc.app in Safari, then tap the Share button — at the bottom of the screen on an iPhone, the top on an iPad.
  2. 2Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3Tap Add in the top right corner.
On Android
Using Chrome
  1. 1Open didc.app in Chrome, then tap the menu button at the top right.
  2. 2Tap Install app — or Add to Home screen if you don't see it.
  3. 3Tap Install to confirm.

Some Android phones skip the menu entirely — the front door shows a one-tap Add DIDC to my home screen button instead. Tap it, then tap Install when your phone asks.

When you're done, the DIDC icon appears on your home screen and opens like any other app. The in-app version of these steps lives on the front door — tap Add this app to my phone and it shows the right steps for whichever phone you're holding.
On a computer?
Everything works in a normal browser tab too — just bookmark didc.app. If you'd like it as an app, look for the install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar and click Install.

Inside a class page
Every class has its own page: the details, notes from the teacher, and any photos, videos or music they've shared.
  1. 1The class card — day and time, teacher, level and studio room, plus length, ages, style and season underneath.
  2. 2Class info — notes posted for this class only: costume details, schedule tweaks, what to bring. If it says "Nothing posted yet", the teacher simply hasn't posted — it fills in over the season.
  3. 3Class content — the files. Photos open full-size when tapped. Videos play right on the page. Music and paperwork download when tapped. The Save link under a photo or video puts a copy on your phone.
Data-friendly
Videos only load a preview frame until you press play, so browsing the page won't eat your data plan. If a file shows "Could not open — try again", it's usually the signal — tap it once more.

Calendar tab · part one
The calendar
Rehearsals, competitions, showcases and closures — everything dated, in a month grid or a simple list.
  1. 1Switch between MONTH and LIST at the top. Month is the grid you see here. List is everything upcoming, in order, grouped by month — past events drop off by themselves. The app remembers which one you prefer.
  2. 2Tap a date with a little bar under it — its events appear below the grid. Today is outlined in pink; the day you tapped is filled in. Multi-day events, like a holiday closure, stretch across the grid as one long bar.
  3. 3Tap the event itself for the full details — when, where, the full write-up, and any attached files.
  4. 4Press ADD to save the date to your own phone's calendar — that's the next page.
Where these events come from
The portal calendar is fed directly from the studio's own calendar, so it is always the latest word. If a date here disagrees with a paper handout, trust the portal.
Browsing the season
The arrows step month by month — up to a year ahead, and one month back. Use LIST when you just want "what's next" without hunting through a grid.

// Which one should I pick?
  • You use Gmail? Pick Google Calendar. It opens with everything filled in — press Save there and you're done.
  • You use the iPhone calendar? Pick Apple Calendar — your phone offers to add the event straight away.

One thing to remember
A saved date is a copy. If the studio later moves the event, the copy in your phone doesn't move with it — the portal calendar is always the source of truth. For anything critical, glance at the portal the day before.

After you tap, a small note at the bottom of the screen confirms what happened — "Opening Google Calendar — press Save there to keep it.", "Calendar file saved — open it to add the date." or "Link copied."

When something's off
Questions & fixes
"The code never arrived."

Check spam and junk first — that is where it usually is. The code is good for a whole hour, so there's no rush. If it really isn't there, the address you signed up with probably isn't the one we have on file: ask at the front desk.

"I've forgotten my password."

On the login screen tap Forgot your password?, type your email and we'll send a reset link. Check spam for that one too.

"It says my email isn't recognised."

Sign-up only accepts the address on your Enrollio account. If you've used a different one — a work address, a partner's — nothing will arrive and nothing will say so. The front desk can tell you which address we hold.

"Where do I pay tuition or update my details?"

In Enrollio, the studio's billing system — tap Billing & Admin at the top of your home screen and it opens in its own tab. Your portal password is separate from your Enrollio one.

"Do I need to update the app?"

Never — it's a website, so you have the latest version the moment you open it.

"A page didn't load or looks stuck."

Usually just the signal. Close the app and open it again — or reload the page in your browser. Everything picks up where you left off.

"A photo or file won't open."

If you see "Could not open — try again", tap it once more — large videos can take a moment on studio Wi-Fi.

"A date on my phone doesn't match the portal."

Saved dates are copies and don't update themselves. Check the portal calendar, then press ADD again to save the corrected one.

"Can I see the other program too?"

Yes — both sections are on your home screen and your login opens either one. All-Stars is the competition team side, Academy / TNT is the class side.

"I'm logged in but I can't see my dancers."

Your account hasn't been matched to your family yet — usually because you signed up with a different address from the one we hold. Tell the front desk and we'll join them up. Payment details always stay in Enrollio.
