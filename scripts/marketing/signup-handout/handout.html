How to create
your account

For families enrolled at the studio. It takes about two minutes, and you only do it once.

Use your Enrollio email

Sign up with the same email address you signed up with in Enrollio — the one the studio already has for you. Any other address will not be recognised, and nothing on screen will tell you so: the code simply never arrives. If you are not sure which address that is, ask us before you start.

1

Open the app and tap Dancer Portal

There are two doors. Families go through Dancer Portal — not Staff Portal.

2

Tap Sign up, then fill in your details

First name, last name, and your Enrollio email address.

3

Choose a password

At least 6 characters. You will type it twice, to catch typos.

4

Enter the 6-digit code from your email

It is sent the moment you tap Create account. Type it in and you are done.
